How It Works

Four clear steps from idea to Grand Finale stage.

1

Apply

Submit your creative work or business idea.

2

Get Validated

Share your entry and attract at least five public votes to qualify for the next stage.

3

Develop

Access bootcamp training, mentorship, and enterprise development opportunities as you qualify.

4

Compete

The top six qualifying entries advance to the Grand Finale, where the panel of judges selects the winners.

Public votes help determine progression, but the grand winners are selected by the panel of judges.
